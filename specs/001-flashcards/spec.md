# Flashcards MVP

## Scope

Personal deck and card management; daily/deck study sessions; Again/Hard/Good/Easy ratings; scheduling; and learning statistics. The authoritative source is [`../flashcards-product-spec.md`](../flashcards-product-spec.md).

## Acceptance checks

- New, learning, review, and relearning scheduling rules are deterministic.
- A successful rating records history and advances scheduling together.
- Suspended and archived content is excluded from the review queue.
- Study can be completed with Space then keys 1–4.
- Arabic and English card sides set their own text direction.
